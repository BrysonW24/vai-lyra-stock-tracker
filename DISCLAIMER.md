# Disclaimer - Research, Not Advice

Lyra (Stock Momentum Radar) is **research and educational software**. It is **not financial,
investment, trading, tax, or legal advice**, and nothing it produces is a recommendation to
buy, sell, or hold any security. Read this before you act on anything you see in the app.

## What Lyra is

- A deterministic momentum scanner. It computes indicators (RSI, MACD, moving averages,
  volume) and a 0-100 momentum-recovery score, and presents them clearly.
- A way to surface names that are **worth a closer look**. Lyra never tells you to buy or
  sell. The decision, and the risk, are always yours.

## What Lyra is not

- **Not a broker or financial adviser.** It does not hold funds, connect to your accounts,
  or place trades.
- **Not a guarantee.** Markets are uncertain. Past momentum does not predict future returns,
  and any signal can be wrong.
- **Not always live data.** Lyra ships with demo and illustrative data. Figures may be
  sample, delayed, or modelled. Private, pre-IPO valuations are editorial estimates, not
  quotes, and are clearly labelled as such.
