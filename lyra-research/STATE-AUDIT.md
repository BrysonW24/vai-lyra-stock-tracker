# lyra-research - State Audit
_2026-07-29 · Lyra v0.89.0 · HEAD 9e5953c · read-only assessment_

## Scope
Customer, product, technical, and market evidence. Today: technical/product research is rich (largely
in `docs/` + `lyra-modelling`); customer/market research is greenfield (pre-users).

## Lyra as it is today
Substantial technical + product research already exists, just not centralised in this folder:
- `docs/dr-charts-for-a-trader.md`, `docs/dr-engagement-finance-app.md` - deep-research pieces.
- `docs/product/small-cap-research.md`, `docs/product/ai-ml-posture.md`, `docs/mobile-packaging-research.md`,
  `docs/horizons.md`.
- **Modelling research** now lives in `lyra-modelling/research/2026-07-29-emerging-winner-engine.md`
  (the Emerging Winner archetype study) plus `lyra-modelling/options.md` and the reference `*.PNG` charts.

Customer/market research (interviews, surveys, competitor-informed demand evidence) is greenfield - Lyra
is pre-users, so there is no primary customer evidence yet.

## How it works
Research is authored as dated docs; the modelling research feeds the score model + the Emerging Winner
Engine bet. There is no research registry or evidence-standards contract in this folder yet (Podium ships
`research-registry.json` + `EVIDENCE-STANDARDS.md`).

## Strengths (verified)
- Deep, specific technical/product research already exists and is actively feeding a live bet.
- The modelling research is honest about status (designed vs building) and keeps the one-law framing.

## Gaps, risks, what is missing
- Greenfield on customer + market evidence (no users yet to research).
- Existing research is scattered in `docs/` + `lyra-modelling`; this folder should index or absorb it.
- No evidence-standards contract to keep research claims disciplined.
